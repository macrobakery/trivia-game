Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 May 2026

The 30-second version

AI Challenge stores your scores, push subscription (if you opt in), and basic usage events to make the app better. We do not show ads, do not sell data, and do not run third-party trackers. Analytics fire only after you tap Accept on the cookie banner.

Who runs this app

AI Challenge is operated as a personal project by Abdulaziz, registered with The Approved Market Central Co. W.L.L., Kuwait City, Kuwait. Contact: macrobakery@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Quiz scores — the player name you type when saving (free-form), the score, level, difficulty, and timestamp. Stored in our Turso (libSQL) database.
  • Anonymous usage events — for example "game_started", "lesson_opened". Tied to a randomly generated browser ID, not your real identity. Only sent if you accept analytics on the banner.
  • Push subscription — only if you tap "Allow" on the browser push prompt. We store the subscription endpoint your browser provides and use it to send daily notifications.
  • Local storage — your streak, theme preference, completed lessons, and consent decision live entirely in your browser. We never read them.

What we don't collect

  • Real names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Payment data — there is nothing to pay for.
  • Precise location, contacts, or device fingerprints.
  • Cross-site tracking. There are no third-party advertising or analytics scripts.

How we use it

  • To run the leaderboard and your personal stats.
  • To send the daily push reminder if you opted in.
  • To understand which features people use, so we can improve them.
  • To prevent abuse (rate limiting + flagged questions).

Where data lives

Scores, subscriptions, and usage events are stored on Turso (libSQL) in the EU/US region. The frontend is served from Vercel's global edge network. Both providers are GDPR-aligned.

Your rights

  • Erase your data — email macrobakery@gmail.com with the player name(s) you used and we'll delete the matching scores and events within 14 days.
  • Disable analytics — clear your browser storage for this site, or click "Decline" the next time the banner appears.
  • Disable push — turn it off in your browser's notification settings.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using the app. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we'll bump the "Last updated" date and post a banner at the top of the home page for at least 7 days.
